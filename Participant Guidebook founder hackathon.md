## **3\. Code of Conduct** {#3.-code-of-conduct}

This is a day for building cool stuff, meeting great people, and having fun. Let's keep it that way.

The short version: Be kind, be respectful, don't be a jerk.

The longer version:

We're committed to providing a welcoming and safe experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, nationality, or experience level.

**What we expect:**

* Treat everyone with respect and kindness — participants, organizers, sponsors, mentors, and judges alike  
* Be collaborative. Help each other out.  
* Be mindful of your language — both at the venue and on Discord  
* Respect personal boundaries and space  
* Accept constructive feedback gracefully, whether it's on your code or your pitch  
* Ask for consent before taking photos or recording others

**What's not okay:**

* Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome comments, intimidation, or sustained disruption  
* Discriminatory jokes or language, even if "it's just a joke"  
* Unwanted physical contact or sexual attention  
* Publishing others' private information without permission  
* Being destructive with venue property or shared equipment  
* Any behavior that makes someone feel unsafe or unwelcome

If you experience or witness anything that makes you uncomfortable, reach out to an organizer — in person at the venue or via DM on Discord. You won't be judged for reporting something, and we'll handle it quickly and discreetly.

**Consequences:**

Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to stop immediately. Organizers may take any action they deem appropriate, including a warning, removal from the event, or disqualification from judging.

## **4\. Rules** {#4.-rules}

This hackathon is about building and shipping in one day.

### **OK to Bring**

* **Ideas, notes, sketches, research** — come prepared with your concept and planning materials  
* **Generic starter templates** — boilerplate you reuse for any project (not project-specific)  
* **Third-party tools, open source, sponsor tech** — use any tools and libraries (respect licenses)

### **Not OK**

* **A mostly finished product** — no bringing pre-built projects and "just polishing" on-site  
* **Project-specific pre-built code** — features built before the hackathon that make up your submission's core  
* **Continuing an existing startup** — build something new and use the day to test a new startup idea, not to continue an existing one

### **What We Judge**

We judge what you build at the hackathon. Projects that are largely pre-built may be penalized in scoring or removed from awards.

Projects are judged on four equally weighted criteria (25% each): Problem & Customer, Product, Business Potential, and Team & Pitch. See How You're Evaluated for details.

### **Best Practices**

* **Commit early, commit often** — use git throughout the day, create your repo at kickoff  
* **Ship a working demo slice early** — get something functional first, then improve. Stability wins.


### **Teams**

* 1–4 people per team  
* Come alone or with a team

## **5\. Before You Show Up** {#5.-before-you-show-up}

This is a founders hackathon: be a founder for a day. You start at 10:00 with an idea and finish with a working product, real customer conversations, an investor pitch deck and, optionally, a live landing page. Do these before Saturday so you can start building right away:

* **Register on Luma** — use the email you will check in with at the venue. You also need that same email to claim the ElevenLabs perk on the Discord form.  
* **Join the ElevenLabs Discord (https\://discord.com/invite/VnBvbbcdEC) — so you can claim your free Creator-tier perk with the Discord bot (see Sponsor Tools & Credits).**  
* **Set up your accounts** — GitHub, YouTube or Google Drive for your pitch \+ demo video, a tool for your slide deck and, if you want one, a landing page tool with a sign-up form (see What to Submit).  
* **Sort out your team** — teams of 1–4, come alone or with a team. Looking for teammates? Post in [Discord](https://discord.gg/hCMfYGbcQ).  
* **Pick a problem and a target customer** — who exactly has the problem, how do they solve it today, and why does that suck?  
* **Talk to potential customers** — Problem & Customer is one of the four judging criteria. Start conversations now and line up people you can reach on the day.  
* **Bring a new idea** — use the day to test a new startup idea, not to continue an existing one.  
* **Prepare generic boilerplate only** — starter templates are fine, project-specific code is not (see Rules).  
* **Pack your laptop and charger** — breakfast, lunch from Compleat, and drinks are on us.

## **7\. What to Submit** {#7.-what-to-submit}

This section covers the first-round submission only. One submission per team: the team lead submits in the hackathon portal once your team is locked.

### **Required**

* **Pitch deck** — your slides (see Pitch deck below for a suggested structure)  
* **GitHub repo** — public, or with access for judges  
* **Pitch \+ demo video** — one video, max 3 minutes: your pitch followed by a product demo. Host it on YouTube (unlisted is fine) or Google Drive (shared as “anyone with the link can view”). Loom, Vimeo and Drive folders are not accepted.

### **Optional**

* **Product link** — your working product (if it's behind a login, put test credentials in the repo README)  
* **Landing page** — a live landing page, ideally with a sign-up form  
* **Customer learnings** — what you learned from talking to potential customers

A missing optional item doesn't count against you, but a broken link you submitted does.

### **How & when**

* **Where:** the hackathon portal dashboard, [https\://www\.creators-ecosystem.de/founders-hackathon](https://www.creators-ecosystem.de/founders-hackathon)  
* **Who:** the team lead, once per team, after your team is locked (teams of 1–4; teams lock at 11:30 Berlin time)  
* **Deadline:** 17:00 sharp (Berlin time), Saturday 7 November 2026\. Late submissions can't be accepted. We recommend you stop developing by 16:45.  
* **What you fill in:** team name, project name, a short description of what you built, plus the links above  
* **Links:** every link must be public when you submit (a private GitHub repo needs access for judges). Judges open them, so private or broken links can't be scored.

### **Pitch deck**

**What to Put in Your Slides (max 5\)**

Goal: Prove this solves a real problem for real users and could become a startup. 

*This is for inspiration only, you can structure your slides as you wish. Just remember: the pitch deck is required and is the main evidence for judging.* 

**Slide 1: The Problem**

* who has this problem? (be specific: "busy founders, freelance designers with more than 10 clients, .." not "businesses")  
* how do they solve it today and why does that suck?

**Slide 2: Your Solution**

* what you built in one sentence  
* why is your approach 10x better than alternatives?

(Keep it short. Just enough to understand what it does.) 

**Slide 3: Product & validation**

* Show us what you built, your live landing page (if you made one), and what you learned from talking to real users. Show, dont tell. 

**Slide 4: Go-to-Market Plan**

Who are your first customers, and how will you reach them?

Did anyone sign up on your landing page (if you made one) or ask to try it?

**Slide 5: Why You / Why Now**

* why are you the right team to build this?  
* would you work on this full-time if it got traction?

### **How You're Evaluated**

We judge your project on the four criteria from the event page: problem & customer, product, business potential and team & pitch. Ship something real. The further you get on the journey during the hackathon, the more points you earn.

The first round is scored by the jury against these four criteria, right after the 17:00 deadline. AI notes may appear beside your submitted materials to help the jury, but they do not include scores and they are not used for ranking. Make sure every link works, because a missing or unwatchable video counts as missing evidence. The top 8 teams from the first round advance to the live finals; details will be shared on the day. Jury for the live finals: Rayn Subramaniam (Co-founder, Unschool, YC W'21), Manuel Szedlak (Founder & CTO, MARSSTEIN), An Dang (AI Engineer at Lufthansa) and Michael Ströbel (lawyer specialising in startups at Luther).

| Weight | Category | What We Look For |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 25% | **Problem & Customer** | Is the problem real, and did you validate it with real people? Who exactly has the problem, proof you talked to real potential customers, and what you learned or changed because of it. Judged from your pitch deck, plus optional landing page sign-ups. |
| 25% | **Product** | Did you build something that works and solves it? A working product, how complete and stable it is. Judged from your product link, your GitHub repo and your pitch \+ demo video. |
| 25% | **Business Potential** | Who pays and how much, how big is the market, why now, and why you beat the alternatives. Judged from your pitch deck. |
| 25% | **Team & Pitch** | Can this team execute, and do you present your idea clearly? In the first round this is judged from your pitch deck and your pitch \+ demo video. |
